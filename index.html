ABOUT MYWEB-SITE

This project started as a learning exercise and quickly became something more personal - my subtle protest against the idea that working in tech means leaving your personality at the door.

Built from scratch in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, this portfolio is where my cloud security skills, projects, and overall journey will reside, alongside the attention to detail and color I've always carried.

Tech doesn't have to be dull or monotonous, because behind every infrastructure, every system, every project, there is a creative human mind that is anything but boring. The design is not just decorative, but also my testament that technical ability and style are not antonyms.
